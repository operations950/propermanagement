QuickBooks accounts for each rental

Every short-term rental runs through two QuickBooks accounts. The income-statement account is its reimbursable-expense account (expenses we pay are coded to it; the monthly reimbursement comes back into it). The balance-sheet account is the owner's trust account (deposits in; direct expenses, reimbursements to us, commission and owner payments out). Choose both for each rental; an account can belong to one rental only.
