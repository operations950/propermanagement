Import bookings

Drag today's Airbnb or VRBO export onto its box below, or click to browse. One file covers your whole portfolio — new reservations, date/detail changes, and cancellations are all handled from the same drop, and each row is matched to the right property automatically (a listing name it hasn't seen before will ask you to confirm it once, then remembers it from then on). Nothing is saved until you review and confirm the changes on the next screen.

Airbnb ships cancellations as a separate export from its regular reservations report — both get dropped onto the same Airbnb box below, just at different times, so make sure both are part of the regular pull.
