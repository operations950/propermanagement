Recurring visits

Standing rules for anything that happens on its own schedule — deep cleans, inspections, a weekly commercial common-area clean, whatever doesn't come from a booking file. Each generates a new visit automatically once its interval elapses (see the visit type's own checklist for what's on it). For a multi-unit property, add one rule per unit — leave unit blank for a rule that covers the whole property.
